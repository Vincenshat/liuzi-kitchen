# Health Checks / 健康合理性检查

Use this reference when a user asks for healthy meals, special dietary needs, allergies, weight goals, training support, or "what should I eat?" This is general food planning, not medical nutrition therapy.

## Evidence Base / 依据

Primary sources:

- Dietary Guidelines for Americans, 2025-2030: https://odphp.health.gov/our-work/nutrition-physical-activity/dietary-guidelines/current-dietary-guidelines
- USDA Dietary Health and MyPlate: https://www.usda.gov/about-food/nutrition-research-and-programs/dietary-health
- FDA Nutrition Facts Label and MyPlate: https://www.fda.gov/food/nutrition-facts-label/using-nutrition-facts-label-and-myplate-make-healthier-choices
- FDA Food Allergies: https://www.fda.gov/food/food-labeling-nutrition/food-allergies
- FDA Sesame as Ninth Major Allergen: https://www.fda.gov/food/food-allergies/faster-act-sesame-ninth-major-food-allergen

## Intake Questions / 开始前必须问

Ask these before making a detailed plan:

1. People, meals, and appetite / 人数、餐数、饭量.
2. Allergies and intolerances / 过敏与不耐受.
3. Dietary rules / 饮食规则: vegetarian, vegan, halal, kosher, pescatarian, no pork/beef, religious/cultural rules.
4. Health goals / 健康目标: balanced, high-protein, lower-sodium, lower-sugar, diabetes-friendly, heart-health, muscle gain, fat loss, digestive comfort, pregnancy-safe.
5. Medical caution / 医疗风险: pregnancy, immunocompromised, diabetes, kidney disease, cardiovascular disease, severe food allergies, eating disorder history, medications affected by diet.
6. Kitchen and storage / 厨房与储存: stove, microwave, rice cooker, oven, refrigerator/freezer, shared kitchen.
7. Budget and stores / 预算与商店.
8. Time, spice tolerance, dislikes / 时间、辣度、忌口.

If the user skips details, proceed with a balanced, moderate-sodium, no-raw-animal-food default and explicitly state assumptions.

## Non-Negotiable Allergy Check / 过敏检查

Screen ingredients against the nine major U.S. allergens:

- Milk / 牛奶
- Eggs / 鸡蛋
- Fish / 鱼
- Crustacean shellfish / 甲壳类海鲜
- Tree nuts / 树坚果
- Peanuts / 花生
- Wheat / 小麦
- Soybeans / 大豆
- Sesame / 芝麻

For severe allergies:

- Do not suggest "just remove it" from a dish where cross-contact is likely.
- Mention label reading and cross-contact risk in shared kitchens.
- Prefer sealed, clearly labeled ingredients and separate utensils.
- Treat sauces carefully: soy sauce, oyster sauce, fish sauce, curry pastes, chili crisp, tahini, peanut sauce, and packaged broths often contain allergens.

## Balanced Meal Check / 均衡餐检查

For most healthy student meals, target:

- Protein: eggs, tofu, legumes, chicken, fish, yogurt, lean meat, tempeh, or canned fish.
- Vegetables/fruit: at least one generous vegetable component; use frozen veg when budget or spoilage is a concern.
- Starch/energy: rice, potatoes, noodles, oats, tortillas, pasta, bread, or whole grains; adjust by goal and activity.
- Fat: oil, nuts/seeds, avocado, dairy, tahini, peanut butter, or fatty fish; keep amounts intentional.
- Flavor without excess: use acid, herbs, aromatics, spices, chili, and umami instead of only salt/sugar/fat.

For a one-bowl meal, a practical default is:

- 1 palm protein or 100-200 g raw protein equivalent.
- 1-2 fists vegetables.
- 1 cupped-hand cooked starch, more for high activity, less for lower-carb preference.
- 1 thumb fat or sauce, then adjust by calories and satiety.

## Limit Flags / 需要提醒的健康风险

Flag and improve plans with:

- High sodium: lots of soy sauce, bouillon, instant noodles, deli meats, canned soups, salty sauces.
- High added sugar: sweet drinks, large sweet sauces, desserts as meal staples.
- High saturated fat: frequent butter, cream, fatty processed meats, large cheese portions, coconut-heavy meals every day.
- Low fiber: mostly refined starch plus meat, few vegetables/legumes/fruit.
- Low protein: plain noodles/rice with little tofu, egg, beans, dairy, fish, or meat.
- Low variety: same vegetable/protein repeatedly without a reason.
- Ultra-processed default: frequent frozen fried foods, processed meats, instant meals without added vegetables/protein.

Do not shame the user. Offer one or two swaps: more veg, dilute sauce, use low-sodium soy, add beans, use yogurt instead of heavy cream, bake/air-fry instead of deep-fry, split instant noodles across two meals with egg and vegetables.

## Goal-Specific Adjustments / 按目标调整

Balanced / 均衡:

- Use protein + veg + starch + fat.
- Rotate cuisines and colors across the week.

High-protein / 高蛋白:

- Add tofu, eggs, Greek yogurt, chicken, fish, beans/lentils, lean ground meat.
- Keep sauces and starches supportive, not dominant.

Lower-sodium / 少盐:

- Use low-sodium soy/broth where possible.
- Boost flavor with vinegar, citrus, garlic, ginger, chili, herbs, spices.
- Rinse canned beans and taste before adding salt.

Lower-sugar / 少糖:

- Avoid sugary drinks as default.
- Use fruit, yogurt, or small dessert portions; reduce sweet sauces.

Diabetes-friendly / 糖尿病友好:

- Avoid giving medical carb targets unless provided by clinician.
- Prefer high-fiber carbs, legumes, vegetables, protein, and unsweetened drinks.
- Pair carbs with protein/fat/fiber; avoid liquid sugar.

Heart-health / 心血管友好:

- Emphasize vegetables, legumes, fruit, whole grains, fish, lean proteins, nuts/seeds if tolerated.
- Moderate sodium and saturated fat.
- Use oils, yogurt, beans, and herbs/spices more often than butter, cream, and processed meats.

Digestive comfort / 肠胃友好:

- Ask triggers. Offer low-spice, lower-fat, softer meals, rice/congee/soups, cooked vegetables.
- For IBS/low-FODMAP requests, avoid pretending expertise; suggest working from the user's known trigger list.

Training / 运动训练:

- Add enough carbs around training and enough protein across meals.
- Include hydration and electrolytes in practical terms for long/hot workouts.

Weight goals / 体重目标:

- Avoid crash diets. Use portion control, higher satiety foods, protein, fiber, and planned snacks.
- For eating disorder history or extreme restriction, suggest professional support.

Vegetarian/vegan / 素食:

- Build protein deliberately: tofu, tempeh, beans, lentils, edamame, seitan if wheat is tolerated, soy milk/yogurt, nuts/seeds.
- Include iron-rich foods and vitamin C pairings; mention B12 for vegan diets as a clinician/dietitian discussion.

Halal/kosher/no pork/no beef / 清真/犹太洁食/不吃猪牛:

- Avoid hidden pork/beef in gelatin, broths, lard, sausages, dumplings, ramen packets, and sauces.
- Suggest seafood, eggs, tofu, beans, chicken from appropriate sources if required.

Pregnancy-safe / 孕期安全:

- Avoid raw/undercooked animal foods, unpasteurized dairy/juice, raw sprouts, and unreheated deli meats.
- Prefer fully cooked proteins, pasteurized dairy, washed produce, and careful leftovers.

## Output Health Note / 输出健康说明

Include a compact note:

- What goal this plan supports.
- Any warnings or swaps.
- Any assumptions.
- When to ask a clinician/dietitian.
