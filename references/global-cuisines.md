# Global Cuisines / 全球菜系

Use this reference to recommend international dishes that are practical for students abroad. Treat each cuisine lane as a flexible cooking language: use its aromatics, acid, fat, starch, and finishing moves while keeping food safety and grocery cost in view.

## Cross-Cuisine Pantry Bridge / 跨菜系共享食材

These ingredients cover many cuisines and reduce waste:

| Ingredient / 食材 | Works in / 可用于 | Notes / 备注 |
|---|---|---|
| Eggs / 鸡蛋 | Chinese, Korean, Japanese, American, Mediterranean | Cook fully for higher-risk users |
| Chicken thighs / 鸡腿肉 | Most cuisines | Cheap, forgiving, cook to 165 F / 74 C |
| Tofu / 豆腐 | Chinese, Korean, Japanese, Southeast Asian, fusion bowls | Refrigerate opened tofu |
| Ground meat / 肉馅 | Chinese, Mexican, Middle Eastern, pasta, rice bowls | Ground meat 160 F / 71 C; ground poultry 165 F / 74 C |
| Canned beans / 罐头豆 | Mexican, Mediterranean, Middle Eastern, American | Low-cost protein; rinse to reduce sodium |
| Rice / 米 | Chinese, Korean, Japanese, Southeast Asian, South Asian, Latin American, Caribbean | Cook extra; cool leftovers fast |
| Pasta / 意面 | Italian, American, Mediterranean, fusion | Good emergency starch |
| Tortillas / 墨西哥饼 | Mexican, wraps, breakfast, leftovers | Freeze extras |
| Cabbage/carrot/onion / 包菜胡萝卜洋葱 | Almost all cuisines | Cheap, long fridge life |
| Frozen vegetables / 冷冻蔬菜 | Almost all cuisines | Low waste; add near end |
| Tomato paste/canned tomato / 番茄膏/罐头番茄 | Italian, Mexican, Middle Eastern, Indian-ish, stews | High flavor per dollar |
| Yogurt / 酸奶 | Mediterranean, Middle Eastern, South Asian, breakfast | Use pasteurized; keep cold |

## Cuisine Lanes / 菜系路线

### Chinese Regional / 中式与地方风味

Flavor base / 风味底:

- Aromatics: garlic, ginger, scallion, chili, Sichuan peppercorn.
- Umami: soy sauce, oyster sauce, doubanjiang, fermented black bean, mushroom.
- Acid/fresh finish: Chinkiang vinegar, rice vinegar, pickled mustard greens, scallion.
- Texture: wok-style browning, crisp-tender veg, silky cornstarch-thickened sauce.

Student dishes / 留子友好菜:

- Tomato egg rice / 番茄炒蛋盖饭: eggs, tomato, rice; cheap, safe, fast.
- Mapo-ish tofu / 家常麻婆豆腐: tofu plus optional ground meat; stretch with mushrooms.
- Cabbage pork noodles / 包菜肉丝面: cabbage as the value anchor.
- Scallion ginger chicken bowl / 葱姜鸡饭: batch-cook chicken and sauce separately.
- Fried rice cleanout / 冰箱清空炒饭: use cold rice; reheat leftovers to 165 F / 74 C.

Shopping kit / 采购包:

- Soy sauce, vinegar, oyster sauce or mushroom sauce, doubanjiang/chili crisp, cornstarch, rice/noodles, eggs, tofu, cabbage, scallions.

Safety / 安全:

- Do not rely on meat color in stir-fries; thermometer-check the thickest piece.
- Cool rice quickly; fried rice must be thoroughly reheated.

### Japanese-Korean / 日式韩式

Flavor base / 风味底:

- Japanese: soy sauce, mirin-style sweetness, miso, dashi/bouillon, sesame, rice vinegar.
- Korean: gochujang, doenjang/miso, garlic, scallion, sesame oil, kimchi, gochugaru.
- Finishes: sesame seeds, nori, scallion, pickles, soft herbs.

Student dishes:

- Bibimbap-ish bowl / 简化拌饭: rice, egg, veg, ground meat/tofu, gochujang sauce.
- Kimchi tofu stew / 泡菜豆腐锅: tofu, kimchi, egg, canned tuna or pork optional.
- Teriyaki chicken bowl / 照烧鸡饭: soy-sugar-ginger glaze; chicken to 165 F / 74 C.
- Miso noodle soup / 味噌汤面: miso, noodles, tofu, greens, egg fully cooked if needed.
- Onigiri/tuna rice bowl / 饭团或金枪鱼饭: use canned tuna, mayo, soy, nori.

Shopping kit:

- Gochujang or miso, soy sauce, sesame oil, rice vinegar, kimchi, rice, eggs, tofu, cucumbers, frozen spinach/broccoli.

Safety:

- Kimchi is acidic but not magic: keep refrigerated after opening.
- Avoid raw egg toppings for higher-risk users; use jammy/hard-cooked eggs.

### Southeast Asian / 东南亚

Flavor base:

- Aromatics: garlic, shallot/onion, ginger/galangal, lemongrass if available, chili.
- Salty/umami: fish sauce, soy sauce, shrimp paste optional, bouillon.
- Sweet-sour: lime, tamarind, vinegar, sugar.
- Fat: coconut milk, peanut, oil.
- Herbs: cilantro, basil, mint, scallion.

Student dishes:

- Thai basil chicken-ish / 打抛鸡饭: ground chicken/pork/tofu, basil or cilantro, rice.
- Peanut noodles / 花生酱拌面: peanut butter, soy/fish sauce, lime/vinegar, cucumber.
- Coconut curry / 椰奶咖喱: curry paste/powder, coconut milk, chicken/tofu, frozen veg.
- Vietnamese-ish rice bowl / 越式饭碗: protein, rice, cucumber, pickled carrot, fish sauce-lime dressing.
- Fried rice with pineapple/egg / 菠萝蛋炒饭: use canned pineapple sparingly for sweetness.

Shopping kit:

- Fish sauce or soy sauce, lime or vinegar, peanut butter, coconut milk, curry paste/powder, rice/noodles, cucumbers, carrots, cilantro.

Safety:

- Fish sauce is salty but raw meat marinades still need boiling before reuse.
- Seafood should be cooked to 145 F / 63 C or opaque/firm depending on type.

### South Asian / 南亚

Flavor base:

- Aromatics: onion, garlic, ginger, chili.
- Spices: cumin, coriander, turmeric, garam masala/curry powder, mustard seed optional.
- Body: tomato, yogurt, coconut milk, lentils, chickpeas.
- Finish: lemon/lime, cilantro, yogurt.

Student dishes:

- Red lentil dal / 红扁豆咖喱: lentils, onion, tomato, spices; very cheap.
- Chickpea curry / 鹰嘴豆咖喱: canned chickpeas, tomato, spinach, rice.
- Chicken curry / 鸡肉咖喱: chicken thighs, curry powder, tomato/yogurt; chicken to 165 F / 74 C.
- Egg curry / 鸡蛋咖喱: hard-boiled eggs simmered in sauce.
- Spiced potato wrap / 香料土豆卷: potatoes, peas, tortillas.

Shopping kit:

- Rice, lentils, canned chickpeas, canned tomato, onion, garlic, ginger, curry powder/garam masala, yogurt, frozen spinach.

Safety:

- Keep cooked rice and dal chilled promptly; dense pots cool slowly, so use shallow containers.
- Yogurt-based sauces should not sit out.

### Mediterranean / 地中海

Flavor base:

- Aromatics: garlic, onion, oregano, rosemary, parsley, dill.
- Acid: lemon, red wine vinegar, pickles, olives.
- Fat: olive oil, yogurt, tahini.
- Umami: tomato paste, feta/parmesan, anchovy optional, mushrooms.

Student dishes:

- Greek-ish chicken bowl / 希腊鸡肉饭: chicken, rice, cucumber, tomato, yogurt sauce.
- Chickpea salad wrap / 鹰嘴豆卷: chickpeas, cucumber, tomato, yogurt/tahini.
- Tomato tuna pasta / 番茄金枪鱼意面: canned tuna, tomato, garlic, pasta.
- Shakshuka-ish eggs / 番茄炖蛋: eggs poached until set in tomato sauce.
- Lemon herb potatoes + protein / 柠檬香草土豆配蛋白.

Shopping kit:

- Pasta/rice/tortillas, canned chickpeas/tuna/tomatoes, yogurt, lemon, cucumber, tomato, garlic, herbs, olive oil.

Safety:

- Cook eggs until yolks and whites are firm unless the user accepts risk and is not high-risk.
- Refrigerate tuna after opening.

### Middle Eastern And North African / 中东与北非

Flavor base:

- Aromatics: onion, garlic, cumin, coriander, paprika, cinnamon, allspice, harissa.
- Body: chickpeas, lentils, tomato, yogurt, tahini.
- Finish: lemon, parsley, mint, pickles.

Student dishes:

- Mujadara / 扁豆洋葱饭: lentils, rice, caramelized onions; very cheap.
- Harissa chicken bowl / 哈里萨鸡肉饭: chicken, rice, roasted/frozen veg.
- Chickpea tomato stew / 鹰嘴豆番茄炖菜.
- Kofta-ish meatballs / 简化肉丸: ground meat, spices, yogurt sauce.
- Hummus plate / 鹰嘴豆泥拼盘: use canned chickpeas; add egg/veg/pita.

Shopping kit:

- Rice, lentils, chickpeas, tomato paste, yogurt, tahini, lemon, cumin, paprika, parsley, pita/tortillas.

Safety:

- Ground meat mixtures need 160 F / 71 C; ground poultry needs 165 F / 74 C.
- Do not leave hummus/yogurt plates at room temperature for long meal prep blocks.

### Mexican And Latin American / 墨西哥与拉美

Flavor base:

- Aromatics: onion, garlic, cumin, oregano, chili powder, smoked paprika.
- Acid/freshness: lime, salsa, pickled onion, cilantro.
- Body: beans, tomato, corn, rice, tortillas.
- Fat: avocado, cheese, crema/yogurt, oil.

Student dishes:

- Bean and egg tacos / 豆子鸡蛋塔可: tortillas, eggs, beans, salsa.
- Chicken rice burrito bowls / 鸡肉墨西哥饭碗.
- Turkey/beef taco skillet / 肉馅塔可锅: ground meat, beans, corn, rice.
- Black bean quesadilla / 黑豆芝士饼: pan, tortillas, cheese, beans.
- Picadillo-ish potatoes / 拉美土豆肉末: ground meat, potato, tomato, spices.

Shopping kit:

- Tortillas, rice, canned beans, salsa, canned tomato, eggs, ground meat/chicken, frozen corn, cabbage, cilantro, lime.

Safety:

- Ground meats require thermometer checks.
- Keep salsa, cheese, and cooked fillings refrigerated; reheat fillings separately.

### African And Caribbean / 非洲与加勒比

Flavor base:

- West African-ish: onion, tomato paste, ginger, chili, peanut, bouillon.
- Ethiopian-ish: onion, garlic, ginger, berbere-style spice, lentils.
- Caribbean-ish: allspice, thyme, garlic, scallion, chili, lime.
- Finish: lime, herbs, pickles, hot sauce.

Student dishes:

- Peanut stew / 花生炖菜: peanut butter, tomato, sweet potato, greens, chicken/tofu.
- Jollof-ish rice / 简化西非番茄饭: rice, tomato paste, onion, spices, veg/protein.
- Red lentil berbere-ish stew / 香料红扁豆炖菜.
- Caribbean jerk-ish chicken bowl / 加勒比香料鸡饭: chicken, rice, cabbage slaw.
- Rice and peas / 椰香豆饭: rice, beans, coconut milk.

Shopping kit:

- Rice, canned beans, peanut butter, tomato paste, onion, garlic, ginger, sweet potatoes, cabbage, coconut milk, thyme/allspice/curry powder.

Safety:

- Peanut stews and rice dishes are dense; cool in shallow containers.
- Chicken must reach 165 F / 74 C even when dark marinades make color hard to judge.

### American And European / 美式与欧洲家常

Flavor base:

- American student: garlic/onion powder, mustard, hot sauce, cheese, pickles, barbecue sauce.
- Italian-ish: garlic, olive oil, tomato, basil/oregano, parmesan.
- French-ish: onion, butter/oil, mustard, herbs, vinegar.
- Eastern European-ish: cabbage, potato, onion, dill, yogurt/sour cream.

Student dishes:

- Chili / 辣豆肉酱: beans, ground meat optional, tomato, spices.
- One-pot mac and peas / 一锅芝士豌豆面: pasta, milk, cheese, peas.
- Garlic tomato pasta / 蒜香番茄意面.
- Mustard chicken potatoes / 芥末鸡肉土豆.
- Cabbage potato egg hash / 包菜土豆鸡蛋锅.

Shopping kit:

- Pasta, potatoes, canned tomato/beans, cheese, milk/yogurt, eggs, cabbage, frozen peas, mustard, herbs.

Safety:

- Dairy sauces need refrigeration.
- Casseroles and leftovers reheat to 165 F / 74 C.

## Fusion Without Chaos / 融合但不乱

Use one cuisine's sauce logic at a time. Fusion works best when only one axis changes:

- Chinese mapo tofu + pasta: keep mapo sauce, change starch.
- Korean gochujang + tacos: keep taco structure, use gochujang ground meat and slaw.
- Mediterranean chickpea bowl + rice: keep Mediterranean sauce, use rice instead of pita.
- Indian dal + tortillas: keep dal flavor, use tortilla as bread substitute.

Avoid mixing many strong fermented condiments unless there is a clear bridge: soy + miso, soy + gochujang, tomato + cumin, yogurt + lemon, peanut + chili.

## Weekly Variety Template / 一周不腻模板

For 5 meals, choose:

1. One rice bowl / 一个盖饭: Chinese, Korean, Mexican, Mediterranean, or Caribbean.
2. One soup/stew / 一个汤或炖菜: dal, peanut stew, chili, kimchi tofu stew.
3. One noodle/pasta / 一个面: peanut noodles, tomato tuna pasta, cabbage pork noodles.
4. One wrap/taco / 一个卷饼: chickpea wrap, bean tacos, spiced potato wrap.
5. One cleanout meal / 一个清冰箱: fried rice, hash, quesadilla, soup noodles.

This gives variety while sharing groceries.

## Global Recommendation Output / 全球推荐输出

When recommending global dishes, include this compact table:

| Cuisine / 菜系 | Dish / 菜 | Why it works / 为什么适合留子 | Buy / 买什么 | Safety / 安全 | Taste key / 好吃关键 |

Keep dishes realistic: fewer rare ingredients, fewer steps, clear leftovers plan, and strong substitutions.
