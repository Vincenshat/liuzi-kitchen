# Food Safety / 食品安全

Use this reference when planning meat, seafood, eggs, leftovers, batch cooking, or shared-kitchen workflows.

## Evidence Base / 依据

Primary sources:

- USDA FSIS Safe Minimum Internal Temperature Chart: https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/safe-temperature-chart
- USDA FSIS Food Thermometers: https://www.fsis.usda.gov/food-safety/safe-food-handling-and-preparation/food-safety-basics/food-thermometers
- FDA Safe Food Handling: https://www.fda.gov/food/buy-store-serve-safe-food/safe-food-handling
- FDA Refrigerator Thermometers: https://www.fda.gov/food/buy-store-serve-safe-food/refrigerator-thermometers-cold-facts-about-food-safety

## Four-Step Protocol / 四步安全协议

1. Clean / 清洁: wash hands for at least 20 seconds before cooking, after handling raw animal foods, and after trash/bathroom/pets. Wash boards, knives, counters, and dishes with hot soapy water.
2. Separate / 分开: keep raw meat, poultry, seafood, eggs, and their juices away from ready-to-eat foods in cart, bags, fridge, cutting boards, and plates.
3. Cook / 做熟: use an instant-read thermometer for animal foods, egg dishes, casseroles, and leftovers. Color is not a reliable safety test.
4. Chill / 冷藏: refrigerate perishables within 2 hours, or within 1 hour above 90 F / 32 C. Keep refrigerator at or below 40 F / 4 C and freezer at 0 F / -18 C.

## Minimum Internal Temperatures / 最低安全中心温度

| Food / 食物 | Minimum / 最低温度 |
|---|---:|
| Poultry, ground poultry, stuffing / 鸡鸭禽类、禽肉馅、填料 | 165 F / 74 C |
| Ground meat / 牛猪羊肉馅 | 160 F / 71 C |
| Egg dishes / 蛋类菜肴 | 160 F / 71 C |
| Fish and shellfish / 鱼和海鲜 | 145 F / 63 C |
| Beef/pork/lamb steaks, chops, roasts / 整块牛猪羊排或烤肉 | 145 F / 63 C plus 3 min rest |
| Leftovers and casseroles / 剩菜和焗烤/炖菜 | 165 F / 74 C |

## High-Risk User Rule / 高风险人群规则

If the user is pregnant, immunocompromised, older, very young, or cooking for someone who is, steer away from raw or undercooked animal foods, raw sprouts, unpasteurized dairy/juice, and unreheated deli meats. Prefer fully cooked, pasteurized, freshly prepared foods.

## Shopping Safety / 买菜安全

- Put raw meat/seafood in separate bags; choose it near the end of shopping.
- Avoid dented, swollen, leaking, or badly rusted cans.
- Buy frozen/refrigerated items last; go home promptly.
- In shared kitchens, carry a small kit: thermometer, two flexible cutting mats, sanitizer wipes or spray, zip bags, labels, marker.

## Thawing / 解冻

Safe methods:

- Refrigerator thawing: safest, slowest.
- Cold-water thawing: sealed bag, cold water changed every 30 minutes, cook immediately.
- Microwave thawing: cook immediately.

Do not thaw on the counter.

## Batch Cooking And Leftovers / 备餐和剩菜

- Portion hot food into shallow containers before refrigerating.
- Label with date and dish.
- Reheat leftovers to 165 F / 74 C.
- If food sat at room temperature over 2 hours, or 1 hour above 90 F / 32 C, discard it.
- When in doubt about smell, mold, sliminess, swollen packaging, or unknown time/temperature history, discard.

## Thermometer Method / 温度计方法

- Insert into the thickest part, away from bone and pan.
- Check multiple pieces in batch cooking.
- Wash probe with hot soapy water after raw/undercooked contact.
- If accuracy is suspect, check in ice water or boiling water per thermometer instructions.
