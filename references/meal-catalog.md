## Recommendation Heuristics / 推荐规则

- Beginner + cheap + safe: tomato egg rice, bean pasta, tofu rice, dumpling soup.
- High protein: chicken bowl, turkey taco bowl, tofu plus egg, tuna pasta.
- Shared kitchen: one-pot rice cooker meals, soup noodles, sheet-pan meals with foil.
- Batch cooking: mapo-ish tofu sauce, taco meat/beans, tomato pasta sauce, roasted chicken/potatoes.
- Low waste: cabbage, carrots, onions, frozen vegetables, eggs, tofu, canned tomatoes.

## Pantry Starter Kit / 留子厨房基础装备

Minimum tools:

- Instant-read thermometer, kitchen scale, chef's knife, cutting board or two flexible mats, pan, pot, rice cooker or microwave-safe bowl, storage containers, labels/marker.

Minimum pantry:

- Rice/noodles/pasta, eggs, tofu or beans, frozen vegetables, cabbage/onion, soy sauce, vinegar, oil, salt, sugar, garlic/ginger, chili oil or hot sauce, bouillon, cornstarch.
